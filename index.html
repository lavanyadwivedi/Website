about

hi, i'm lavanya.

I'm a BBA student at Amity University Mumbai who has been building brands online since 2024. I research the audience first, then make content that fits them, from the first idea to the finished reel.

I've grown a minimalism brand's reach past 5.9 million, brought in 1,540 leads in two weeks for a career platform, and helped a hearing clinic get found online. I also run my own thrift store, so I know what it takes to sell, not just to post.
